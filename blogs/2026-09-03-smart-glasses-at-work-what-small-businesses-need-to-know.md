Have you thought about the fact that your employee’s glasses could be recording you right now? Smart glasses look like ordinary frames, but they can pack in a camera, a microphone, and an AI assistant that captures whatever the wearer’s looking at. That opens up some exciting possibilities for employees, particularly around accessibility.
